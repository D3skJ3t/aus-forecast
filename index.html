    Boating conditions

    Rough guide for jet skis and small boats, from the strongest daytime wind (6am to 6pm): calm under 15 km/h, choppy 15 to 25, rough above 25. Gusts, swell and local conditions matter too; check the Bureau of Meteorology's marine warnings before heading out.
